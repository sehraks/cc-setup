# Privacy Policy — CC

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

CC (“the Bot”) is a Discord bot designed to support community management and everyday server activities. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you interact with CC, why it may be used, and what choices you have.

> **Draft notice:** This is a starting-point policy, not legal advice. Before relying on it, the operator should verify it against CC's current code, database schema, logs, hosting, and third-party integrations, and fill in any missing operator/contact details.

## 1. Information CC may process

Depending on which features are enabled and how you use CC, information may include:

- **Discord identifiers:** user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and message IDs needed to identify users, servers, or Discord resources.
- **Server and feature settings:** configuration values selected by server administrators, such as designated channels or roles and feature preferences.
- **Moderation and activity records:** warnings, timeout or moderation actions, offense history, and information needed to operate commands or protect a server.
- **Content you submit:** command inputs, messages, feedback, reminders, confessions, or other content you intentionally provide to a CC feature.
- **Technical and diagnostic information:** error details and operational logs needed to maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the Bot. These logs may contain identifiers or parts of command inputs.
- **Web panel sign-in information:** when you use the Guild Panel, Discord OAuth may provide basic profile information and the servers you are allowed to manage, as permitted by the authorization scopes shown during sign-in.

CC does not need your Discord password. Never provide passwords, bot tokens, API keys, or other secrets in chat or support requests.

## 2. How information may be used

Information may be processed to:

- Provide the commands and features you request.
- Apply server settings and carry out moderation or administrative actions authorized by a server.
- Save feature settings or records that need to persist between uses.
- Authenticate eligible users to the Guild Panel and determine which servers they can access.
- Prevent abuse, investigate errors, maintain security, and improve reliability.
- Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 3. Messages and third-party services

Some CC features may process content you submit, including through AI-backed replies or other connected services. When a feature uses a third-party provider, the relevant content may be sent to that provider to fulfil your request and will also be subject to that provider's own terms and privacy policy.

The exact providers and data handling may change as features evolve. The operator should document the current providers and their applicable policies before publishing this document as final. Avoid sending sensitive personal information to bot commands or integrations.

## 4. When information may be shared

Information may be accessible to:

- **Server administrators and authorized moderators**, where information is visible through Discord or through moderation and server-management features.
- **Service providers** that host, operate, or support CC, but only as needed for their services.
- **Third-party integrations** used to fulfil a feature you choose to use.
- **Authorities or other parties**, where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

CC does not make information public intentionally through this policy. However, content posted in a Discord server may be visible to members according to that server's settings and permissions.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Information may be retained for as long as needed to operate a feature, preserve server configuration, maintain moderation history, troubleshoot issues, or meet legal and security needs. Retention periods can vary by data type and feature.

Deleting a Discord message or leaving a server may not automatically delete information already stored by CC. To ask about deleting stored information, contact the Bot's operator through the official support route listed in the CC documentation or Discord community. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operational, safety, or legal reasons.

**Operator action required before publication:** Specify actual retention periods and deletion procedures for moderation records, logs, reminders, confessions, panel sessions, and any stored user or server data.

## 6. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored information and access to the Bot's infrastructure. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not share credentials or sensitive information with CC or anyone claiming to provide support.

## 7. Children's privacy

CC is intended for use within Discord and must be used in accordance with Discord's age requirements and applicable law. Do not use CC if you are not permitted to use Discord or the relevant service in your location.

## 8. Your choices and requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. You can also choose not to use a feature or revoke the Guild Panel's Discord authorization through Discord's settings. Revoking authorization may not delete information already stored by CC.

To make a privacy request, contact the operator through the official support route linked from the CC documentation. Do not include passwords, tokens, or other secrets in your request.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as CC's features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of CC after an update means you have had an opportunity to review the revised policy; it does not remove any rights you have under applicable law.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official CC support route linked from the [CC documentation repository](https://github.com/sehraks/cc-setup).
